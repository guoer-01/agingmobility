census_block_group: GEOID of the Census Block Group (CBG)
social_child_type: the elderly-specific service	
num_accessible_facility: the number of POIs providing the service within the accessible area (a 5-mile radius from the CBG center)	
demand_visits: the number of visits from the CBG to POIs providing the service
demand_ratios: the ratio of the number of visits from the CBG to POIs providing the service to the population in that CBG
demand_met_in_local: the revealed service accessibility, A
